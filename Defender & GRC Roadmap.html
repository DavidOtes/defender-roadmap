Defender & GRC Roadmap
12-month plan · Blue Team + GRC

From graduate to defender

One technical track that gets you hired as a SOC analyst, with a governance, risk and compliance layer built alongside it. That combination fits how Nigerian banks and fintechs actually hire under the CBN cybersecurity framework and NDPA.

Pace: 10–15 hrs/weekFits around: NYSCFirst job target: SOC Analyst L1 / IT Risk Analyst

The strategy

Core

One shared foundation

Security+ covers the ground both tracks stand on: threats, controls, risk, governance. You study it once and it serves both.

Blue Team

Skills that get the first job

SOC roles hire juniors in volume. Hands-on log analysis, SIEM and incident response make you employable fastest.

GRC

The layer that sets you apart

A defender who can map an alert to an ISO 27001 control or a CBN requirement is rare, and that's the path into risk, audit and leadership roles.

Phase 1 · Foundations & Security+

Months 1–3Core

Goal: pass Security+ (SY0-701) and get comfortable with hands-on basics.

CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701)List price $439 from CompTIA as of June 2026; authorized resellers are cheaper. Valid 3 years. If you sit it in 2027, check whether a newer exam version has replaced SY0-701.

Phase 2 · Networks, systems & your home lab

Months 3–5Blue Team

Goal: understand what "normal" looks like so you can spot what isn't.

Phase 3 · SOC analyst skills

Months 5–8Blue Team

Goal: investigate alerts like a working analyst and earn a practical blue-team cert.

Blue Team Level 1 (BTL1): recommendedA 24-hour practical incident-response exam covering phishing, threat intelligence, forensics, SIEM and IR. The cert doesn't expire. It costs around £399; check Security Blue Team's site for current pricing and any regional discounts.
CompTIA CySA+: alternativeMultiple choice plus simulations. Better at getting past HR keyword filters, weaker at proving hands-on skill. Take it if the job adverts you're targeting ask for it by name.

Phase 4 · The GRC layer

Months 6–9 (overlaps Phase 3)GRC

Goal: speak the language of frameworks, risk and regulators, especially the Nigerian ones.

ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Implementer (PECB or similar)Widely recognised by Nigerian banks, Big 4 advisory and consultancies. Local training providers in Lagos run it, so compare them on price. If budget is tight, start with the cheaper ISO 27001 Foundation.

Phase 5 · Portfolio & job hunt

Months 9–12Both

Goal: turn everything above into interviews.

Keep in mind

  • NYSC: try to get posted to, or redeployed to, a bank, telco, government IT/audit unit or consultancy. Even a non-security IT role there gives you real logs, real audits and references.
  • Documentation is the job. SOC and GRC both run on clear writing. Every lab you do should end with a written report.
  • Don't collect certs for the sake of it. Security+, BTL1 and ISO 27001, plus a strong portfolio, beats five multiple-choice certs.
  • Verify prices before paying. Exam fees change often and many have regional or reseller discounts.
